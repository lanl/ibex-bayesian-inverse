18. \textbf{Reviewer 1:} \textit{Section 3.2. Since the surrogate outputs are
high-dimensional, would a multivariate measurement of performance be more
appropriate? RMSE and CRPS are univariate measures.}

\bigskip
\noindent
We appreciate the reviewer’s thoughtful suggestion. We agree that a
multivariate scoring rule, such as the energy score, could provide additional
information about the joint predictive performance of the surrogate for these
high-dimensional outputs. However, calculating such a score requires a
representation of the joint predictive distribution. In our setting, this
would require either estimating and storing the full high-dimensional
predictive covariance matrix or generating joint predictive draws that
preserve this covariance structure. Both approaches would be computationally
prohibitive for the problem considered here. Moreover, some of the competing
methods provide only marginal predictive distributions and do not produce the
joint covariance information or joint predictive draws needed to calculate a
multivariate score. Therefore, we are unfortunately unable to provide such a
metric.
